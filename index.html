Hello I'm

Carlos Quijano-Chavez

I'm a Ph.D. student at the University of Stuttgart, Germany, under the supervision of Prof. Michael Seldmair and Prof. Dieter Schmalstieg. My current research focuses on the emerging topic of Situated/Immersive Analytics, which focuses on applying engaging, embodied display and interaction techniques for data visualization and visual analytics in augmented/virtual reality. During my master's journey, my research focused on how best to enable 3D data exploration in immersive virtual environments under the supervision of Prof. Carla M. D. S. Freitas and Prof. Luciana Nedel.
