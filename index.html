Carlos Quijano-Chavez

Ph.D. Student in Computer Science · University of Stuttgart (VISUS)

Computer Science researcher and developer specializing in HCI, virtual/augmented reality, and software development. I am currently a PhD student at the University of Stuttgart under the supervision of Dieter Schmalstieg and Michael Sedlmair, focusing on Situated and Immersive Analytics. Previously, I earned my master's degree at the Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS), where my dissertation focused on applying embodied display and interaction techniques for data visualization and visual analytics in immersive environments.
